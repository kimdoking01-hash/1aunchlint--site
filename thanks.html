Thanks — your key is on its way

Two steps and you are done.

1. Find the key

Polar emails the license key to the address you paid with. It looks like LNCH-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. You can also open your Polar customer portal from the same email and copy it from your purchases page.

2. Activate it in the extension

  1. Open LaunchLint and press the gear icon in the panel header (or right-click the toolbar icon → Options).
  2. Paste the key into License key and press Activate license.
  3. The badge in the panel turns to Pro. Client reports and white-label headers are unlocked.

Using it on another machine?

A license covers three devices. If you replace a laptop, press Deactivate this device on the old one first, or free the slot from your Polar customer portal, then activate on the new machine.

Something wrong?

Mail kimdoking01@gmail.com with the order id from your Polar receipt. Keys are not stored on our side, so the receipt is the fastest way to look you up.
