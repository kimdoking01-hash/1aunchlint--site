Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

LaunchLint is a Chrome extension that audits the web page you are currently viewing and turns the result into a report. This policy explains exactly what the extension reads, where it goes, and what it never does.

The short version

  • The page you scan is analysed inside your browser. Its content, screenshots and results are never uploaded to us.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising SDK, no tracking pixel and no account sign-in.
  • The only network request the extension makes is a license-key check with Polar (polar.sh) when you activate or re-check a paid license.
  • Scan results and settings are stored in your browser's own extension storage, on your device.

What the extension reads

When you press Scan this page, LaunchLint reads the page that is open in the active tab:

  • Page metadata: title, meta description, canonical URL, robots directives, language, viewport, social-preview tags.
  • Page structure: headings, links and their targets, images and alt text, form fields and their labels, duplicate ids.
  • Rendered text and computed colour values, used to check contrast.
  • Browser performance timings already available for that page load (time to first byte, load time, resource sizes, third-party origins, Largest Contentful Paint).
  • HTTP status codes for up to a handful of same-site links on that page, requested from your browser.

This reading happens only when you ask for a scan. LaunchLint does not run in the background, does not crawl other pages, and does not read pages you have not scanned.

What leaves your device

Page content never leaves your device. The only outbound request is the license check described below, which contains your license key, the product's organization id and a short device label.

RecipientWhenWhat is sent
Polar Software Inc. (polar.sh), our merchant of record — their privacy policy When you activate a license, when the extension re-checks it (at most once a day), or when you deactivate this device License key, organization id, benefit id, device label, and the standard HTTP request metadata (IP address, user agent)

We do not receive, store or process that payment or license information ourselves beyond what Polar's customer portal shows you.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: your agency name, accent colour and display preference.
  • License record: the key you entered, the activation id Polar returned, and when it was last checked.
  • Scan history: the page URL, score and issue counts of your last few scans (no page content).

This data lives in Chrome's extension storage for this profile. You can clear the scan history from the extension's settings page, and removing the extension deletes all of it.

Data we never collect

  • No browsing history, no list of open tabs, no keystrokes, no clipboard.
  • No page HTML, screenshots, form entries or passwords.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data, ever.

Children

LaunchLint is a tool for professional web work and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date, and material changes are noted in the extension's release notes.
